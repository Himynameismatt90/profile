My personal favourite! A Japanese language assistant to help you study. As someone who enjoys consuming Japanese media, it can be rather frustrating having to pause a movie, a game or a book to look up words and grammar. So that's why we made PeraFlash!

MoodWalk

Feeling stressed? Want to go outside to clear your head but don't know where? MoodWalk has you covered! The app will decide a route for you based on your location and what you want to get out of your walk. But that's not all!

On your walk, you can capture something you thought was cool, moving, or interesting and keep it for later. At the end of your walk, your photo will be saved on a card for you to look back on, when you want to reminisce about a certain time. Your route and memory can be shared with the community, helping others enjoy a relaxing walk and lead them to discover their own special moment!
